The Details

Dietary Restrictions

We're finalizing our dinner menu now! If you have any food allergies or need a vegetarian option, please text or email Chaerin or Dasol by September 13th so our kitchen can take care of you.

Dress Code

Semi-formal to formal — cocktail dresses, suits, that kind of vibe. No specific color theme, so come in whatever makes you feel your best!

Just a heads up: our ceremony is outdoors on a slightly sloped grassy lawn (everything else is indoors). The ground may be soft, so stilettos could sink or get muddy — we suggest block heels or flats so you can walk and dance comfortably.

Parking

Senior parking: The spots right next to the restaurant are very limited and reserved for our senior guests.

Main guest parking: We've rented the lot at the nearby fire station ( 13889 Skyline Blvd, Woodside, CA 94062 ), just a 3–5 minute walk to the venue. Please park there and take care walking over!

Drinks & Getting Home

Alcoholic beverages will be flowing throughout the reception and dance party. We want everyone to let loose and get home safely — please consider carpooling or taking an Uber/Lyft.
